From process to structure

Mathematical objects are understood here as stabilized constructions, synchronizable with discrete time and collective memory.

Gardens as suspended processes

A garden is not bounded by explicit borders. What may appear as a boundary is in fact a process of contraction, relative to a situated pair of belief and assurance. In this sense, the notation |.| expresses a stabilized tension rather than a terminal mark.

The garden is suspended. Its traits are drawn vertically, not as static symbols, but as traces of a canonical drift process. Each drift adds a trait, progresses by a quarter turn of an axis, and may emerge above or below its previous position.

Lianas, drift, and alternation

Modules are not autonomous carriers. They are supported by lianas, along which drift occurs. A liana supports an alternation between ATM and RTM viewpoints, or the reverse.

What matters is not a binary value, but whether a liana is cut or not. This distinction corresponds not to true or false, but to belief versus assurance: a confidence in being, or a confidence in the impossibility of non-being, relative to a situated center.

From gardens to enriched graph structures

When read as a stabilized relational structure, a garden may be interpreted as a graph. This interpretation, however, requires an explicit enrichment of the notion of edge.

Edges are not simple undirected links. Each edge carries two local labels: one at its departure and one at its arrival, each relative to a situated community center. These labels need not coincide.

Moreover, edges may be interrupted. A pending edge carries only a single label, corresponding to an open or ongoing process. Such edges are not defects, but express incomplete synchronization or suspended drift.

Depending on which aspects are retained or forgotten, the same garden may further stabilize into abstract graphs, planar graphs, or directed and decorated variants. Each stabilization corresponds to a community choice, not to an intrinsic structure of the garden.
